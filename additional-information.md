### KIBERTOCHKA CS2 SEASON 2 — Additional Information

---

## Tournament Operator
- **Tournament Operator:** Kibertochka

## Tournament Format
- **Open Qualifiers:** online (CISPRACS), 15–22 July 2026 — 8 qualifiers, one per day, start 12:00 MSK. Single Elimination, Best-of-1 (semifinals Best-of-3), unlimited sign-ups. Top 2 teams from each qualifier advance to the Closed Qualifier (16 teams total).
- **Closed Qualifier:** online, 26–28 July 2026 — 16 teams, Single Elimination, all Best-of-3 (Round of 16 → quarterfinals). The 4 quarterfinal winners advance to the LAN main stage.
- **LAN Main Stage:** 27–30 August 2026 — 8 teams (4 direct VRS invites + 4 from the Closed Qualifier), Double Elimination, Best-of-3.
- **Venue:** Cyber Lounge The Mansion (https://t.me/themansion_msk), Moscow, Friedrich Engels St. 21 (metro Baumanskaya).

## VRS Information
- **Tournament Tier:** Tier 2 (with Direct VRS Invites)
- **Ranked Stages:** HLTV.org, after our formal application, will determine which stages and matches will count toward VRS. Ranked status is not guaranteed until confirmed.
- **VRS list used:** Filtered ranking by countries: Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, and Georgia.
- **Number of Direct VRS Invites:** 4
- **VRS rank from which invitations begin:** from the top of the filtered VRS list, downward, without skipping, until 4 teams accept.
- **VRS publication used for invitations:** Invitation VRS — August 2026.
- **VRS publication used for seeding:** Invitation VRS — August 2026.
- **Invitation date:** early August 2026. Invited teams confirm by 18 August 2026.

## Seeding and Invite Resolution
- **Seeding:** all stages are seeded using the Invitation VRS — August 2026.
- **Invite order:** Direct VRS Invites are issued sequentially from the declared filtered VRS list, top-down, without skipping, in accordance with the Valve Tournament Operation Requirements.
- **VRS priority:** rosters without a VRS rank are not seeded above VRS-ranked rosters.
- **Fallback:** if Direct VRS Invite slots remain unfilled after all eligible teams from the declared list have been invited sequentially (including declines and ineligibilities), the remaining slot(s) are filled from the Open/Closed Qualifier pipeline.
